It was nerve-racking but ultimately a fun experience. I'm grateful to the engaging discussion with my opponent, Assoc. Prof. Frederik Hjorth, and my committe whose questions pushed my thinking further. Thanks to my supervisors, Hanna Bäck and Jon Polk, and to everyone who supported me throughout my PhD journey!
