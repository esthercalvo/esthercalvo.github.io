I ran my very first 5 km race at the Malmö Midnattloppet and set a new personal best. I’m so proud of what I accomplished, especially considering I could barely run for a minute last year. It was really fun running with friends and having others cheer us on, even when we were struggling. I’m definitely planning to run again next year, and hopefully, I’ll even do the 10 km!
