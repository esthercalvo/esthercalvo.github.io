Hi, I'm Esther! I earned my PhD in Political Science at Lund University in April 2025. Prior to coming to Lund, I taught in the Department of Social Sciences at the University of the Philippines Los Baños from 2014 to 2020. My main research interests include computational text analysis, legislative behavior and speech-making, and party competition.

Outside of work, I’m often exploring Malmö’s food scene, hunting down my next favorite book, or out for a run — always curious, whether it’s with data or dinner.
